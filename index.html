How it works

  1. Food Input: you select a food
  2. Dataset: nutrition data is loaded
  3. Processing: Python cleans and scales the data
  4. AI/ML: K-Nearest Neighbors finds similar foods
  5. Result: calories, protein, carbs and fat
  6. Budget Option: cheaper alternatives are suggested
  7. Website: results and tips are displayed
